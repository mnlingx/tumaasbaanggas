Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This site shares Philippine fuel-price updates. We aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

Information you provide

There are no accounts or contact forms on this site. If you email us at tumaasba@tumaasbaanggas.com, we receive the email address and information you choose to include so we can read and respond to your message.

Site data and cookies

The site loads its published fuel-price data from a local data file. This site itself does not set or read cookies, use browser local storage, or run analytics or advertising trackers. The web-hosting provider may process technical connection information as part of delivering the site; refer to the provider's privacy information for details.

External sites and donations

News references link to external publishers. Donation QR codes may take you to payment services. Those services have their own privacy practices, which apply when you visit or use them.

Contact

For privacy questions, email tumaasba@tumaasbaanggas.com.
